# Cheaper Than Forecast

> [!example] Example signal. It draws on three notes and says something none of them says alone — that's the test. A real signal should have more evidence than this.

## Summary
Two unrelated technologies — heat pumps and on-device AI — are getting cheaper or more capable faster than expected, and in both cases the driver is not the one forecasters model.

## Evidence
**2026-10-01.** Heat pump costs down for a third quarter, with fitting time named as the driver — [[Heat pump installation costs falling faster than forecast]]. Laptop-sized AI models good enough for routine drafting — [[Small AI models now run well on a laptop]].

## So what
If forecasts keep under-calling the pace, plans that wait for a forecast "tipping point" will start late. The consequence for the focus: check which of your plans depend on a published cost forecast, and how old it is.

## Assessment
Maturity 4, impact 6.0, strength `emerging`. Only two evidence notes plus one concept note; the cross-sector pattern is suggestive, not established. Would be falsified by cost falls stalling once hardware prices stop dropping.
