# T1 · Faster Than The Forecasts

> [!example] Example theme — delete or rewrite once you have real signals.

## Description
Across several technologies, real-world costs are falling ahead of the forecasts that planning documents rely on.

## Insight
The forecasts are not just wrong on numbers; they are wrong on *mechanism*. They model hardware cost curves, but the falls are coming from installation speed and software. That makes the falls harder to reverse than the forecasts would suggest.

## Tension
Cheaper does not mean adopted. If the real bottleneck is skilled labour or planning consent, faster cost falls may not bring adoption forward at all — and the theme would be describing prices, not change.
