## Core idea
Installed costs for domestic heat pumps are falling faster than the published forecasts assumed, driven more by installer productivity than by cheaper hardware.

## Supporting evidence
- [Installer survey: costs down for third quarter running](https://example.com/heat-pumps-q3) — confirms the direction; first source to name fitting time as the driver. *(2026-10-01)*

- [Trade body: average job now under two days](https://example.org/fitting-times) — independent domain; supports the productivity explanation. *(2026-06-14)*

## Why this matters
If cost falls come from labour productivity, they are less exposed to hardware supply shocks than forecasts assume — and adoption could arrive earlier than planning documents expect.
