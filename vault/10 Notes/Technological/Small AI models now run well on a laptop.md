# Small AI models now run well on a laptop

> [!example] Example note — delete once you have real ones

## Core idea
Small language models that run on an ordinary laptop are now good enough for routine summarising and drafting, so "AI" no longer has to mean "send the data to a cloud provider".

## Supporting evidence
- [Benchmark roundup: laptop models close the gap](https://example.com/local-models) — the headline claim, with test results. *(2026-05-20)*
- [Vendor ships on-device assistant by default](https://example.net/on-device) — a commercial product, not just a benchmark. *(2026-07-08)*
- [University study on summarisation quality](https://example.edu/summaries) — independent evaluation; finds the quality gap is small for short documents. *(2026-08-11)*
- [IT buyers survey on local AI](https://example.org/it-survey) — demand side: buyers cite privacy, not cost. *(2026-09-18)*

## Why this matters
Privacy reviews have been the slowest step in most AI pilots. If the model never leaves the device, many of those reviews get simpler.
